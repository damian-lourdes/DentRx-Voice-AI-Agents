Privacy Policy

DentRX — a product of Progspectra LLC. Last updated August 21, 2026.

This policy explains how Progspectra LLC ("we," "us") handles information collected through DentRX, an AI phone assistant used by dental practices to help patients book appointments, cancel appointments, and check insurance claim status by phone.

Information we collect

When you call a practice using DentRX, we collect the information you provide during the call, which may include your name, phone number, appointment preferences, and insurance member ID. Calls may be recorded and transcribed to provide and improve the service.

How we use it

We use this information only to carry out the request you made on the call — booking or cancelling an appointment, or checking a claim status — and to send you a text message confirmation of that action. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Text messages

If you provide a phone number during a call, we may send you a one-time SMS confirming the details discussed. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of future messages, or HELP for assistance.

Data sharing

Information is shared only with the dental practice you called, and with service providers (such as our telephony, hosting, and messaging providers) as needed to operate DentRX. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Progspectra LLC at the contact information provided by the practice you called.
